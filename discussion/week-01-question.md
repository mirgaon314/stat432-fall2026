title: "Which prerequisite topics matter most here?"
author: "Owen Park (owenp3)"
---

Homework 01 showed me how much of STAT 410 and STAT 425 I no longer remember.
Running the code was fine. The parts that asked me to explain were not: why the
maximum-likelihood variance divides by n while the unbiased estimate divides by
n - p - 1, or why a large condition number lets two coefficients swing by ten
while the fitted values barely move.

My question is about triage rather than review in general. Which prerequisite
topics from probability, mathematical statistics, and linear regression carry
the most weight later in this course, and which ones are safe to pick up as they
appear? If you had one week to rebuild that background before the material gets
harder, what would you review first, and how would you check that you actually
understand it instead of just recognizing it?
